141 photographs in seven collections

I have come to really enjoy the art of photography in the last few years. It all started when my wife, Hillary, and I went on our one year honeymoon around the world. We only had room to carry the packs on our back and thus our souvenirs were the photos we took along the way.

Since that trip I have only gotten more serious about the art of photography. I am happy to say it is my number one passion in life. I hope you enjoy the view of world through my lens.

The photographs here are grouped into seven collections — the cities after dark, the mountains and canyons, the coastline, the year of travelling, and the people and animals who sat still long enough. Nothing here is for sale. It is simply the work I am happiest with.
