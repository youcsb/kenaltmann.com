How this started Seattle, Washington

It all started when my wife, Hillary, and I went on our one year honeymoon around the world. We only had room to carry the packs on our back, and so our souvenirs were the photos we took along the way.

Since that trip I have only gotten more serious about the art of photography. I am happy to say it is my number one passion in life.
